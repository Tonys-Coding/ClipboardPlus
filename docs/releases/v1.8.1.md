# Clipboard+ v1.8.1 — Separate favorites

This patch gives every clip one clear home:

- Favorite clips appear exclusively in **Saved**.
- **All**, **Text**, **Links**, and **Code** show only non-favorite history.
- The All and category counters exclude favorites, while Saved shows the complete favorite count.
- Unfavoriting a clip returns it to All and its matching category automatically.

All existing Clipboard+ functionality remains free and local. No permissions, accounts, analytics,
billing, or remote services were added.

Verification: 108 unit/component tests, type checking, linting, development/store builds and
validators, and both Chromium smoke suites. The development browser suite verifies that a favorite
is absent from All and Text while remaining available in Saved.
