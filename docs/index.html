Private by design

Your history stays in Chrome's local extension storage. Clipboard+ has no account system, analytics, advertisements, or remote clipboard processing in its free v1.7.1 release.

A permanent free core

Everything included through Clipboard+ v1.7.1 remains free. Any future subscription will apply only to new services such as encrypted cross-device sync.
